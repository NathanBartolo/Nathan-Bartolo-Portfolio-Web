The Friend

Project Type Game
React Js CSS Web Design Music Game Development Interactive Narrative Firebase

The Friend is a Full Motion Video (FMV) narrative game created by 5ENSE Studios. Formed by a group of five aspiring students, the project was designed to showcase our individual skills and creative strengths. Over the past three months, we developed an interactive FMV experience where players investigate a 2016 cold case through recovered interrogation footage, navigating the conflicting stories of four friends.

Before the project came to life, there was the team behind it—5ENSE Studios, a collective of unique individuals united by the goal of utilising our creative talents across film, music, technology, and design to reinvent how people navigate digital experiences.

I served as the Lead Developer and Sound Designer for the project. My responsibilities included supervising the game’s coding and backend development, leading technical implementation, and composing and producing the original score and soundtrack that shaped the game’s atmosphere and emotional experience.
